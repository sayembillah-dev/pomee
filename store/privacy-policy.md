# Pomee Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Pomee is a focus timer published by Twodesk. This policy explains what the
app does with your information.

## Information we collect

None. Pomee does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any
personal or usage data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no
network access.

## Information kept on your device

Pomee saves your light or dark theme choice on your device so it is
remembered next time. This setting never leaves your device, except as part
of your phone's own backup if you have that turned on. Uninstalling the app
removes it.

## Device sensors and vibration

Pomee reads the motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) while it is open
to tell when the phone is picked up and to move the hourglass sand. It uses
vibration for the pickup alarm. Sensor readings are processed in the moment
and are never recorded or sent anywhere.

## Children

Pomee does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
