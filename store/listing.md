## App name (30 max)

Pomee: Focus Timer

## Short description (80 max)

A focus timer that fills your screen with water, plus a real sand hourglass.

## Full description (4000 max)

Pomee is a focus timer you can feel. As you work, water slowly rises from the
bottom of the screen until the session is done. Then it turns green and drains
away while you take your break.

PICK A SESSION
Choose 10, 20, 25 or 30 minutes of focus, each with its own break. Tap a time
or drag across the slider, then press play.

PUT THE PHONE DOWN
Pick the phone up during a focus session and Pomee buzzes and flashes until
you put it back. It is a small nudge to stay on task.

A SAND HOURGLASS THAT ACTS REAL
Switch to the hourglass, set a time and flip to start. The sand always falls
toward the ground. Turn the phone over and it flows back. Lay it flat and it
pauses.

CALM BY DESIGN
Light and dark themes, smooth spring animations and large, easy-to-read
digits. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Pomee works fully offline and keeps
nothing but your theme choice on your device.
